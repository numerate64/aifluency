THE BIG IDEA

AI can be a powerful partner.
Your judgment is still the point.

01 / CHOOSE

Know when to bring AI in.

Some work is better done by you. Some gets better with a collaborator. Learn to tell the difference.

02 / GUIDE

Give it something to work with.

Clear goals, useful context, and a little back-and-forth turn vague asks into useful results.

03 / OWN

Keep responsibility human.

Question the answer. Protect people's information. Stand behind what you share or decide.
